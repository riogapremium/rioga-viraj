About New Launch Sewri
Every detail is thoughtfully designed to offer panoramic views, both inside and out. On one side, sweeping vistas of the Eastern Waterfront stretch before you; on the other, the beautifully landscaped Grand Central Forest provides a rare and tranquil retreat within the city.
Rising in Art Deco–inspired grandeur, it pays homage to Mumbai’s rich architectural heritage.
