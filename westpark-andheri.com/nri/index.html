About DLF West Park
DLF Limited proudly introduces its first landmark development The West Park, nestled in the bustling yet serene neighbourhood of Andheri. Spread across a sprawling 18 acres, this exclusive project offers meticulously crafted 3, 4 & 5 BHK homes designed to elevate your lifestyle. Strategically located, the project ensures seamless connectivity to business hubs, entertainment zones, and lifestyle avenues, making it the perfect destination for modern living in Mumbai.
