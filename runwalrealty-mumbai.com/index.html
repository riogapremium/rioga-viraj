Crafting Excellence in Every Square Foot
Runwal Enterprises is one of Mumbai’s leading real estate developers, with a strong presence across residential, commercial, and retail segments. Established under the legacy of the Runwal Enterprises (founded in 1978), the company has delivered 50+ landmark projects and served over 48,000 families. With a portfolio of ~48.7 million sq. ft. including luxury residences, townships, and iconic retail spaces like R-City Mall, Runwal Enterprises continues to shape Mumbai’s skyline. Backed by robust growth, strategic partnerships such as with HDFC Capital, and a strong project pipeline, the brand is recognized for its innovation, quality, and trust.
