About Prestige Group
Founded in 1986, Prestige Group is a premier real estate developer which has successfully delivered a plethora of landmark developments across major cities in South India including Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kochi, Mysore, Mangalore, and Goa. It boasts of a widely diverse portfolio including the residential, commercial, retail, leisure and hospitality segments. The Group has introduced many firsts to South India’s real estate market and has become a name synonymous with innovation. It is a CRISIL DA1 rated developer and has won many awards and accolades.
