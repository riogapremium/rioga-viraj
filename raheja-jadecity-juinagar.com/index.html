About Raheja Jade City
K Raheja Corp Homes introduces Raheja Jade City, a landmark 55-acre integrated township in Juinagar featuring premium 2 & 3 BHK residences. Designed as a Live-Work-Learn-Play destination, it harmoniously blends luxury homes, workspaces, learning hubs, and leisure avenues. see more... With contemporary architecture, lush green spaces, and lifestyle amenities, it offers a balanced, future-ready urban living experience. Strategically located in the heart of Juinagar, it ensures seamless connectivity to Mumbai’s prime business districts, educational centers, and transport networks.

About K Raheja Corp
K Raheja Corp Homes is a distinguished name in India's real estate sector, renowned for its luxury residential developments that blend architectural excellence with sustainable practices. As the residential arm of the K Raheja Corp, the company has a legacy spanning over four decades, delivering over 10 million square feet of residential space across major cities like Mumbai, Pune, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, and Goa .
