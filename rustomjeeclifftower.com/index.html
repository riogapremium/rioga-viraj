About Rustomjee Cliff Tower
Rustomjee Cliff Tower rises with an elegance that redefines Mumbai’s luxury skyline. This architectural landmark stands on a quiet ledge above the city, offering a world of unmatched privacy and exclusivity. Perfectly poised in a rare setting, Rustomjee Cliff Tower stands on a quiet ledge above the city, open to nothing but sky and sea. A rare collection of premium residences, featuring state-of-the-art 4 & 5 bed abodes, each crafted for those who appreciate elegance, privacy, and unobstructed views.

About Rustomjee Group
Founded in 1996, Rustomjee has redefined Mumbai’s skyline with landmark residential, commercial, and township projects. With over 20 million sq. ft. delivered, the brand is synonymous with design excellence, uncompromising quality, and timely delivery. Blending innovation with sustainability, Rustomjee creates spaces that elevate lifestyles — from iconic luxury towers to vibrant gated communities.
