About Kalpataru Amare
Welcome to Kalpataru Amare, an exclusive residential enclave nestled in the most coveted neighbourhood of Juhu. Offering meticulously designed 3, 3.5 & 4 BHK residences, this luxury development brings together elegant architecture, spacious layouts, and refined finishes, crafted for those who desire an elevated lifestyle. Experience privacy and tranquillity while being surrounded by the cultural richness and vibrancy of Juhu. About Kalpataru Developers
Kalpataru Developers is one of India's leading real estate companies, known for its legacy of excellence and innovation since 1969. With a strong focus on delivering quality and creating landmark projects, Kalpataru has redefined urban living with premium residences, integrated townships, and commercial spaces.
