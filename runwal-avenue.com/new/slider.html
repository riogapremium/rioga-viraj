About Runwal Avenue

Kanjurmarg East, Mumbai

This project is a part of Runwal City Centre, a 36 acre township built on the live, learn work, play theme. Experience urban living at its best. Welcome to Runwal Avenue. A perfect blend of fine living, sought-after comforts, high-street delights and green open spaces. Surrounded by the choicest of comforts, conveniences and excellent connectivity, this will be the venue for numerous happy memories, making it your venue for a good life.
