About K Raheja Corp
K Raheja Corp, with a legacy of over four decades, is one of India’s leading developers known for its diverse portfolio—residences, commercial spaces like Mindspace, hotels like Chalet, and Inorbit Malls. Its residential arm, K Raheja Corp Homes, specializes in premium and luxury housing, delivering distinct and aspirational living spaces. A pioneer in sustainable development, the company partnered with CII-Green Building Council in 2007 to promote green buildings. Many of its projects are certified Gold and Platinum. The brand is also committed to developing vast green expanses across India, earning accolades at prestigious forums.
