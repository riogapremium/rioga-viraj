About Raheja Atlantis
Raheja Atlantis is the newest addition to the iconic Raheja District. It is an integrated township by Raheja Universal that redefines modern urban living in Juinagar, Navi Mumbai. Sprawled across an expansive 68-acre land parcel, this Premium Luxury Development is dedicated to 6 Acres of land & 35 world-class amenities. Offering Exclusive & Large 3 & 4 BHK Homes. This visionary development blends premium residences, retail avenues, and lifestyle spaces into a self-sustained urban ecosystem.
