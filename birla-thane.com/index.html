About Birla Thane
Birla Estates Kalwa, Thane is an upcoming premium residential township spread across ~30 acres along the Thane–Belapur Highway. Designed by Hafeez Contractor, the development offers spacious 2 & 3 BHK luxury apartments with multiple size options, modern layouts, and  see more... breathtaking creek and garden views. Backed by the trusted Aditya Birla Group legacy, the project blends upscale urban living with eco-friendly, nature-inspired design. With 30+ lifestyle amenities, top-notch construction quality, and seamless connectivity to Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, and key hubs, Birla Estates Kalwa redefines elegant, sustainable living in the heart of Thane.

About Aditya Birla Group
With over 125 years of legacy, the Aditya Birla Group entered real estate in 2016 through Birla Estates Private Limited, focusing on premium and mid-income housing. Guided by its LIFEDESIGNED® philosophy, Birla Estates creates spaces that nurture and enrich lives. Headquartered in Mumbai, the company develops residential, commercial, and mixed-use projects across major cities, with a growing portfolio of Grade-A properties and 10+ lakh sq.m under development.
