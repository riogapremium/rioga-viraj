About L&T Green Reserve
L&T Green Reserve, Sector 128 Noida, is a premium residential address that blends natural serenity with cutting-edge urban living. Developed by L&T Realty, the project offers spacious 3, 4 & 5 BHK luxury apartments, thoughtfully designed with airy balconies, smart layouts, and world-class finishes. Spread across lush green landscapes with walking trails, landscaped gardens, and eco-conscious architecture, L&T Green Reserve ensures a lifestyle rooted in sustainability. The residences provide a perfect balance of elegance and comfort, elevating everyday living.

About L&T Realty
