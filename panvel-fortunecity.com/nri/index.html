About Hiranandani Developers
As pioneers of global community townships, The Hiranandani Group continues to transform city landscapes. Decades ago, when Mumbai did not look beyond Bandra and Chembur, we spotted barren quarry land in Powai and foresaw the potential of creating a well-planned township in its place.
We transformed this hillside quarry land into an integrated township of over 250 acres. Today, it is home to a thriving global community, living in harmony with nature. Similarly, we expanded the city's horizon to Thane by developing around 350 acres of land into a thriving global township with world-class amenities.
