About L&T West Square
Experience the stunning beauty of nature in its full, awe-inspiring splendor from the luxurious residences at West Square, L&T Realty’s prestigious residential project in Seawoods, Navi Mumbai. The new tower at L&T West Square Seawoods is situated in the corner section of the plot, surrounded by no other properties, offering you a tranquil and exclusive living environment. These premium towers boast spacious 2, 3, and 4 BHK apartments, each with balconies attached to every room, providing breathtaking views of the garden, lake, and sea from every corner of your home.
