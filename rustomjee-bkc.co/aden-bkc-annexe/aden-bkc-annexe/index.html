About Rustomjee Aden
Rustomjee Aden in BKC Annexe brings you newly launched 3 BHK residences designed for comfort, privacy, and community living. Just minutes from Bandra’s nightlife, the Western Express Highway, and Mumbai’s corporate hubs, it offers unmatched connectivity with the luxury of spacious homes. With rooftop amenities including a swimming pool, gym, yoga deck, party lounge, and kids’ play area, life here is as vibrant as the city outside. At Rustomjee Aden, every detail—from soundproof interiors to panoramic balconies—has been crafted to blend style with convenience, giving you the perfect address at the heart of Mumbai.
